# Iconly assets

The base catalogue comes from the Iconly resources in the native Squared Workspace app.

The directional arrows (Light and Bold), MoreSquare, Show and Play come from Iconly Essential v2, distributed by [react-iconly](https://github.com/jrgarciadev/react-iconly/tree/5cebf6e/website/icons). Only their colour is normalized to black; their SVG geometry is preserved. The distribution licence is included in LICENSE-Iconly-Essential.txt.

The contrast ChevronDown variant keeps the native Iconly geometry and changes black to white for dark select controls.
